# WALLETCHI | THE product | consolidated 2026-07-26 (supersedes SPEC.md, now historical; the former RUNNER-SPEC.md was retired and removed from the repo)

**A Tamagotchi-style digital pet that lives in your Apple/Google Wallet. Free to mint with just an email. Caring for it costs real tokens: 90% burned, 10% into the Pot. Stop caring and it dies. The last pet alive wins the entire Pot. 2000 pets, all on Robinhood Chain mainnet, everything on-chain.**

Scrapped as products (Hunt, 2026-07-26): PUNCHCARD, the cubs, SALMON RUN. Their infrastructure is Walletchi's foundation and stays running where useful; no code deleted. "Tamagotchi" is used descriptively only (Bandai trademark); working brand WALLETCHI (Hunt may rename; keep all naming in theme modules).

## 1. The creature
Chunky pixel-art digital pet: round blobby body, big expressive eyes, tiny limbs; modern-retro LCD-pet energy in the soft palette (river blues/teals, salmon pink, dusk lavender). Born as an EGG at mint; hatches on the owner's FIRST care action (hatch scene + push: the first-touch magic). Moods drive the full card-front art: happy (bounce + sparkles), hungry (drooped, tummy-rumble squiggle), thirsty (tongue out), sad/lonely (rain cloud, glossy eyes), sleeping (night), sick (all needs low: green tinge + thermometer), GONE (survival death: empty scene, paw prints, one ghost flicker farewell). Toys accumulate as persistent scene props (ball, duck, crown). Adorability gate: lead reviews every scene before any phone push.

## 2. The economy (the game)
- Care token: ANY ERC-20 plugged in by address (Hunt launches on Pons, pons.money, and delivers the address; testnet rehearsal uses MockCareToken). Players buy it themselves (Uniswap on Robinhood Chain) plus a little ETH for gas (Across bridge): the site links out, we never custody or swap.
- Actions: FEED / WATER / PLAY / GIVE TOY / EXPLORE. Every action costs careCost tokens pulled from the PET'S OWN 6551 account, split 90% BURNED (0x...dEaD) / 10% to the POT (bps constructor-configurable, treasury 0). Per-action cooldowns.
- Survival: every care action resets the pet's on-chain life timer (LIFE_TTL, owner-tunable in [1h, 30d] until config lock). Timer lapse = death: permissionless reap(tokenId) with a small pot bounty. LAST PET ALIVE claims the ENTIRE Pot, paid to the winning pet's own 6551 account.
- Owners top up their pet by sending care tokens to the pet's own address; free mints seed a starter balance from the creator allocation.

## ENDGAME REDESIGN (audit wave 2026-07-26, econ audit: 4 High ship-blockers with passing PoCs)
Econ audit found the "reap until aliveCount==1" model self-defeating. Fixes (fold with access audit before broadcast; re-audit the redesign):
- H-1 reap drains pot to reapers: REDESIGN endgame. Winner = pet with the MAX lastCareAt (kept alive longest), settled by optimistic claim + challenge window after minting closes. reap() pays NO pot bounty (becomes optional bookkeeping / tiny flat incentive from burn if needed). Pot stays whole for the winner by construction.
- H-2 sole-survivor expiry locks pot forever: winner-by-max-lastCareAt means there is ALWAYS a settleable winner even if all timers lapsed; ADD owner/timelocked rescue as backstop; claim/challenge window measured from mint-close, not a pet's own TTL.
- H-3 early claim bricks 2000 mint: SEPARATE phases: MINT phase (until maxSupply or mint-deadline/owner-close) then SURVIVAL/settlement. Minting NOT gated by potClaimed. Raise minPlayers.
- H-4 relayer-only care = referee + SPOF: ADD owner/holder-callable care (permissionless care allowed since it spends the pet's OWN TBA and only extends life; cooldown-gated). Relayer stays as convenience.
- M-5 fee-on-transfer token desyncs pot: account pot on balanceOf-delta (tokens actually received), not nominal careCost; and/or validate token at deploy.
- L-6 assert careCost*potBps >= BPS in constructor; L-7 add a timelocked owner sweep for stranded/surplus tokens.
Re-run BOTH audits against the redesign. PoCs at scratchpad/audit-econ-poc.

## TAX ECONOMY (2026-08-17, Hunt): buy/sell tax funds holder + player rewards, evolution-weighted, gold-ready
